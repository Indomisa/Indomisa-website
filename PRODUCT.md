## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are small and medium business (SME) owners and decision-makers in South Africa evaluating a custom software development partner. Their job to be done: get a web, mobile, or business system built that actually fits how their business works, rather than forcing operations into generic off-the-shelf tools.

## Product Purpose

Indomisa Consulting designs and builds custom software, web applications, and business systems for SMEs. It exists to help these businesses automate manual processes, improve operational visibility, and scale without being constrained by one-size-fits-all software. Success means a delivered system the client's team actually uses and that measurably reduces manual work or improves a business process.

## Positioning

Deep specialization in small and medium businesses within the South African market specifically — not a generalist agency serving any size or region of client. This focus (local market knowledge, SME-scale engagement, pricing, and support model) is the thing a generic or overseas competitor could not truthfully copy.

## Operating Context

Engagement spans three service lines: business analysis (requirements gathering, process mapping, SRS documentation, MVP scoping, user stories, UAT planning), software development (custom applications, e.g. field service/dispatch style systems), and web development (websites, portals, web applications). Based in Johannesburg, South Africa; area served is South Africa (ZA).

## Product Principles

- Fit over convenience: software should match the business's actual workflow, not force the business to adapt to generic tooling.
- SME-scale and South-Africa-specific: engagements, pricing, and support are sized and localized for small/medium businesses in South Africa, not enterprise or global generalist clients.
- Analysis before code: requirements, process mapping, and scoping are treated as a real deliverable stage, not a formality before development.
- Long-term partnership over one-off delivery: support continues after launch (24/7 support, "long-term partnership" messaging) rather than ending at handoff.
